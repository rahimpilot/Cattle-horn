Cattle Horn sits in the heart of Historic Roseville, where Roseville Road meets Fischer-Hallman Road — farming fields on every side, and a short country drive from Kitchener-Waterloo.

Inside, it is warm by design: a restored heritage building, an indoor fireplace, and a room made for lingering. Outside, when the season allows, the patio opens the evening up to the fields and the dusk sky.

Executive chef Christy Varghese leads a contemporary Canadian kitchen built around premium Canadian ingredients — expertly prepared steaks, fresh seafood, handcrafted pastas, seasonal salads, signature burgers, and chef-inspired features.
